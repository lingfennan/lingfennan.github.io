At Palo Alto Networks I led and contributed to large-scale systems that detect and mitigate real-world DNS threats delivered to and through the Domain Name System:

- **Attacks:** DNS tunneling and exfiltration, DNS relaying, DNS hijacking, and cache poisoning.
- **Misconfiguration vulnerabilities:** dangling DNS records and claimable non-existent domains that enable subdomain takeover and related abuse.

This line of work produced several conference papers (e.g., [IEEE S&P 2025](/publication/2025-05-12-resolution-without-dissent), [DSN 2026](/publication/2026-06-23-dns-relaying), [AsiaCCS 2026](/publication/2026-06-01-ipv4-domain-hijacking)) and numerous [granted patents and applications](/patents/).

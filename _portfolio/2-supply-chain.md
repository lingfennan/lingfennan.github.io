title: "Securing the Open-Source Software Supply Chain"
excerpt: "Large-scale automated vetting of OSS packages to detect and mitigate supply-chain attacks."
collection: portfolio
---

Thousands of malicious open-source software (OSS) packages have been published across popular ecosystems (e.g., PyPI, npm, RubyGems) and downloaded millions of times. Because OSS is the de facto way modern applications are built, such attacks can propagate malware into countless downstream apps and services.

This project built a novel large-scale automated vetting infrastructure to analyze millions of OSS packages and detect supply-chain attacks, supporting malware analysis, detection of undesired behavior in untrusted third-party code, and enforcement of ecosystem security. The work was supported by an NSF SBIR award (No. 2112368).

- Paper: [Towards Measuring Supply Chain Attacks on Package Managers for Interpreted Languages](/publication/2021-02-21-maloss) (NDSS 2021)
